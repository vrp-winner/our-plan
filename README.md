## 📖 Game Overview

**Our Plan** is a turn-based relationship life simulation game where players control a couple striving to build a successful life together within **16 weeks (rounds)**. 

Simulating real-life relationships through strategic decision-making, every choice creates trade-offs across three interconnected core stats: **Relationship**, **Stress**, and **Money**. It’s not about playing perfectly—it’s about holding everything together before it falls apart.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay & Core Mechanics

* **Point & Click Turn-Based System:** Players take turns managing a limited time budget per round. Every activity consumes time and impacts core stats.
* **Three Interconnected Systems:**
  * ❤️ **Relationship:** Love and emotional connection. *(Warning: If Relationship drops to 0, it's Game Over!)*
  * 😫 **Stress:** Mental and physical pressure from work and life events.
  * 💰 **Money:** Financial stability required to pay rent, go on dates, and achieve life goals.
* **Random Objectives:** At the start of each game, couples receive a secret life goal to complete before the 16th round, such as:
  * Getting married
  * Buying a dream house and a car
  * Retiring with $800,000
  * Visiting every dating spot in town

---

## 🕹️ Game Modes

* **👥 Co-op Mode (2 Players):** Two players represent one couple, collaborating to manage time, finances, and emotions to achieve their ultimate goal (requires Relationship > 80 and Stress < 40).
* **⚔️ Competitive Mode (4 Players):** Two couples compete head-to-head in the same town. Win by completing your objective and reaching 100 Relationship first—or if the rival couple breaks up!

---

## 🗺️ World & Activities

Explore different locations around town, each with unique risks and rewards:
* **🏢 Workplace:** Earn money to fund your goals, but increases stress.
* **🏦 Bank:** Deposit/withdraw savings, manage finances, and pay monthly rent.
* **🏠 Apartment / Dream House:** Upgrade your living space as your life progresses.
* **🎡 Dating Locations (Beach, Mall, Restaurant, Theme Park, Museum):** Spend money to boost your Relationship and relieve Stress.

---

## 🎲 Dynamic Random Events

Unpredictable **Global** and **Personal Events** keep every playthrough fresh:
* **Global Events:** *Happy Valentine's* (one free date), *Anniversary Day*, *Bad Weather*, and *Epidemic* (requires buying medicine at the mall).
* **Personal Events:** *Lottery Winner!* (+20K bonus), *Robbery* (lose 25% of personal money), and *Illness* (persists until cured).
